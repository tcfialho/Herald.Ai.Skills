# Qualitative refinement and evaluation

Use for usefulness, clarity, experience, visual design or other qualitative goals; see [shared acceptance](evaluation.md). Numerical checks can support judgment, not substitute for it.

## Translate ambition into observable quality

For "make it excellent", identify purpose, consumer, constraints and the difference between adequate and exceptional. Use the actual artifact and references to define observable inadequate/adequate/excellent anchors, protected strengths and required facts/behavior. Excellence is not more features, decoration, text or similarity alone. Without a reference, use relevant domain practices and stated intent; disclose assumptions rather than invent taste.

Extract useful qualities from a reference (e.g. hierarchy, legibility, information density, material, pacing, compositional rhythm, interaction, tone or logical coherence); identify how they serve the actual purpose. Compare specific regions, states or passages, not generic adjectives. Preserve the requested identity, constraints and functionality; do not imitate the reference literally where it conflicts. If stylistic choices materially depend on unexpressed preferences, use reversible alternatives or ask only about that trade-off rather than silently inventing a preference.

## Inspect the real outcome

Render, read or exercise the actual artifact as its consumer would, including relevant sizes, states and representative/adverse journeys rather than one favorable snapshot. For an interface, inspect readability, hierarchy, spacing, clipping, contrast, feedback and state transitions; choose relevant checks, not every device. For documents, verify facts, contradictions, missing instructions and ability to act; for designs, inspect relationships, coherence and constraints. Use screenshots/output/walkthroughs when available; verify identity/freshness and never claim unseen evidence.

For each material criterion, report observation/location, anchor, before/after difference and remaining defect/impact. Rank the few highest-leverage actions. A critic may find no material defect: do not demand negativity, praise or reward effort. Separate observable failures from taste; recheck serious criticism against evidence before acting.

## Compare alternative outputs

Use the same rubric, cases, scale, conditions and relevant references. Compare actual original/incumbent/candidate, not plans or polish claims. An alternative author/approach can reveal distinct qualities; consider a synthesis as a new candidate only when that preserves its parents' strengths (see [exploration.md](exploration.md)). Conceal author, version and intended changes when possible. Ask for criterion-specific evidence, strengths preserved, material defects, and winner/tie/uncertain verdict. Give evidence before optional scores. Do not give credit for novelty by itself or for matching a numerical proxy.

For close or consequential comparisons, reverse order; use an independent evaluator or fresh context when available and cost-justified. A second pass by the original agent is not independent. If disagreement remains, seek a discriminating output or mark uncertainty; do not vote until the desired version wins. A known-bad control containing a factual defect or missing essential quality can validate that the rubric detects problems. No need to assign scores to subjective judgments.

## Iterate toward real quality

Choose the weakness or unexplored opportunity that most affects the intended experience. Predict a concrete visible/usable difference before editing and identify the protected properties; run/render again. Avoid merely addressing one critique when it creates another defect or removes the essential ambition. If changes repeatedly trade one aesthetic criterion against another or fail to change user-visible outcome, reconsider the reference, mechanism and trade-off. Try a distinct approach with the same intent instead of endless surface polish. Stop on a grounded target or defensible plateau/budget; do not equate reaching a score with excellence. Document what is confirmed, what is judgment, and what remains unobserved.
